Loud fan or overheating? Dust builds up deep inside the PS5 where you can't reach it, and the
liquid metal on the processor can develop dry spots that make it run hot.

We open the console, clean out the dust and build-up, and clean up the liquid metal on the
processor to get rid of the dry spots that cause overheating.
